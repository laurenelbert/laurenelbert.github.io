Design

HungerQ: 2026

This app is for Figbuild 2026 Case Competition. The prompt was: “Identify something intangible, invisible, or previously unmeasurable about the human sensory experience and design a speculative tool to track and influence it. This tool should be designed to support a wellness goal or behavioral change for an individual or a group.” Our app is focused on tracking six main hormones related to hunger. Its main focus is on helping our users better understand signals related to hunger, thirst, satiety, and cravings in order to respond better to their body's needs and build lifelong healthy habits.

"Major Market Events Report: CU Alumni Association: 2025"

Analyzed the performance of CU Boulder Alumni Association’s FY25 “CU in the City” event series across eight major markets. Evaluated attendance, donor engagement, advertising performance, and event ROI to identify opportunities for stronger alumni engagement and volunteer recruitment. The campaign generated 648 registrations, 342 attendees, 170 donors, and 174 new volunteer newsletter sign-ups, demonstrating the effectiveness of targeted event marketing and social media promotion in activating alumni communities.

Branding

"Why I Love Myself" Sophomore Outreach Project

This branding guide establishes the visual identity and messaging framework for the Why I Love Myself (WILM) initiative. WILM is a community outreach program led by students in the Leeds Scholars Program at the University of Colorado Boulder that aims to empower middle school girls and gender nonbinary youth through mentorship, self-confidence, and personal development workshops. By standardizing these visual and messaging principles, the WILM branding guide helps create a recognizable identity that strengthens the program’s mission: encouraging young people to embrace their individuality, build resilience, and grow into confident individuals.
