here is everything i have done, professionally.

I am a Marketing and Business Analytics student at the University of Colorado Boulder with a strong interest in the intersection of data, design, and human-centered experiences. My work spans analytics-driven marketing strategy, UX/UI design, and client-facing operations, with a focus on translating insights into clear, impactful outcomes.

Through academic programs such as Leeds Scholars and Business and Engineering Women in Technology, along with hands-on experience in marketing, CRM management, and digital engagement, I have developed a foundation in data analysis, content strategy, and brand-aligned communication. I have coordinated multi-city events, analyzed audience and engagement data, managed social media platforms with measurable growth, and designed digital prototypes and marketing assets to support organizational goals.

I am especially motivated by projects that combine quantitative thinking with creative problem-solving—whether that means improving user experiences through UX/UI design, strengthening community engagement through storytelling and branding, or leveraging data to inform strategic decisions. My interests center on building thoughtful systems, meaningful client relationships, and well-designed experiences that make everyday interactions more effective and engaging.
